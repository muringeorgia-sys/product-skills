### Listing reconciliation

`source_record_key` resembles `postgres:listing:<id>` or `legacy_mysql:listing:<id>`. For duplicate legacy `ad`/`inactive_ad` copies, the whole `ad` record takes technical precedence. Conflict flags and original source references remain available. This precedence does not establish authoritative sale outcomes.

Canonical make/model IDs are parent-validated. Mileage in recognized mile units is multiplied by 1.609344; unknown units remain unknown. Year must be integral and within the accepted range. Raw values and provenance are retained.

### Grouping V3: `seller_bounded_v3`

The user explicitly requires **different users to remain separate, even when VIN is the same**. Users are source-qualified. Missing seller identities remain singletons. Therefore the estimated vehicle count is not a globally deduplicated count of physical cars across sellers or sources.

Candidate matching stays within source, seller, canonical make/model, manufacture year, exact normalized specification signature, and VIN stream. The signature includes generation, body, fuel, gearbox, steering, drive, color, and positive engine-volume text. Missing specs are not wildcards. VIN-bearing and VIN-free streams are separate.

- VIN-free groups accept an unambiguous listing up to 60 days after the last assigned publication, with whole-group mileage spread ≤1,000 km and price maximum ≤1.10 × minimum.
- Same eligible VIN, seller, and signature has no time/numeric matching limit.
- If multiple groups qualify, the listing remains an ambiguous singleton rather than bridging them.
- Invalid/missing keys and additional matching guards also produce singletons. All listings are retained.
- The vehicle key anchors to the earliest assigned listing. Corrections/backdated data can change assignments and keys; no persistent identity registry exists.

Tests validate implemented invariants and fixtures; they do not prove identity accuracy. Large changes in engine/body/price or movement between sellers can split a real car into multiple estimated groups. Do not call these globally unique registered vehicles.

## 6. Prices, periods, and analytical eligibility

### USD normalization

USD listings remain USD. KGS prices use KGS-per-USD rates associated with listing dates. A manual seed supplies earlier monthly-derived rates and later daily rates; rates are not replaced by the latest exchange rate for all history.

The last confirmed FX seed ended **November 10, 2026**. Extension was not confirmed. Earlier validation found ten PostgreSQL KGS listings without FX. Do not treat that historical count as today's missing-FX total. Earlier monthly rates are an assumption/approximation, not verified daily observations; there is no extrapolation after the daily series ends.

### Stored price versus historical price

`int_vehicle_period_prices` groups stored prices by listing creation/publication period. It is not a time series of all price changes on a listing. A snapshot price associated with a listing created in 2023 is not automatically a verified 2023 asking price.

This affects historical price-trend interpretation. A same-model comparison does not reconstruct missing past edits. Use the provenance label `listing_publication_period`, show snapshot dates where relevant, and avoid claiming longitudinal price history without an actual historical price source.

### Vehicle-period selection

For each estimated vehicle and month/year, select the latest price-eligible listing created in that period; if none qualifies, retain the latest record for accounting. Monthly and annual representatives are selected separately. Do not add monthly and annual rows together.

`period_listing_count` records the number of logical listing publications represented. It differs from the one vehicle-period observation used for the median.

General price exclusions, in first-matching-reason order:

1. Before 2019 reporting start.
2. Listing creation after its source extraction snapshot.
3. Missing canonical make/model.
4. Missing/invalid manufacture year.
5. Manufacture year after publication year.
6. Age above 30 years.
7. Source specification-copy conflict.
8. Missing USD price.
9. Nonfinite USD price.
10. USD price outside 500–500,000.

Use `analysis_price_usd` for eligible medians and retain `stored_price_usd`, `price_eligible`, and `price_exclusion_reason` for audit. General price eligibility does not imply complete eligibility for every metric; mileage and age aggregates have their own valid samples.

Age is **publication calendar year − manufacture year**, not exact elapsed age from registration or production date. A vehicle can consequently move between integer age bins without a measured anniversary.

## 8. Residual-value marts: exact interpretation

### Same-period method

For each model/generation/powertrain and report window:

`RV at age N (%) = median used asking price at age N / median new asking price in the same window × 100`.

This compares different sampled vehicles. It does not follow one physical car through five years. A generation spans multiple manufacture years and can contain several engines, trims, body variants, or markets. Exact generation-string matching does not guarantee identical specification mix.

New baseline: reported-new condition, age 0–1, mileage ≤1,000 km, and all other RV eligibility rules satisfied. Used ages displayed: exactly 1, 2, 3, and 5. Manufacture year and age are evaluated at each selected listing's publication date, not all recalculated at the report end.

RV additional exclusions include missing generation/powertrain/age/condition/mileage; age outside 0–11; mileage outside 0–800,000; zero mileage above age two; damaged condition; reported-new outside ages 0–1; and reported-new mileage above 1,000. General price exclusions also apply. An age inside 0–11 but not 1/2/3/5 need not appear as a displayed used-age column.

Condition mapping: legacy code 4 → new; 1/2/3 → non-new, with damaged code 3 excluded from RV. PostgreSQL normalized `новое` → new; recognized good/ideal/no-investment/accident labels → non-new, with accident excluded from RV. Unknown condition remains unknown. This is an operational proxy, not independently verified vehicle condition.

Within each RV report window, select one estimated vehicle observation: latest RV-eligible monthly representative, otherwise latest representative for exclusion accounting. This starts from already-selected monthly representatives, not all original listing rows. A better RV candidate discarded during monthly price selection cannot be recovered at this stage; assess the impact if selection completeness becomes material.

### Windows

| Mart | Windows |
|---|---|
| `marts.mart_residual_value_calendar` | Calendar years from 2019; current year is partial |
| `marts.mart_residual_value_rolling` | Rolling 12, 24, and 36 months ending at the latest completed quarter boundary |

The November 2026 examples ended on **2026-10-01 exclusive**. The 36-month example covers **2023-10-01 inclusive to 2026-10-01 exclusive**. A wider window increases sample availability but mixes price environments and cohorts. It is not a current-month price estimate.

Calendar cutoff is computed as the current Bishkek date plus one day, not the last successful source extraction time. Always check actual snapshot/FX freshness; a report-end label alone does not prove source coverage. Never pool the whole history into an unlabeled price median to address thin samples.

### Samples, statuses, and admission

The latest threshold is n ≥11 for each displayed price. A ratio requires both new and corresponding used samples ≥11. Only one age needs to qualify for a model to have some supported RV; do not require all four ages or remove all partially supported rows.

| Field/status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `raw_new_price_usd`, `raw_price_age_N_usd` | Retained calculated medians, including thin samples |
| `new_price_usd`, `price_age_N_usd` | Display prices gated by the current sample threshold |
| `new_n`, `n_age_N` | Estimated selected vehicle observations; not every reposted listing and not verified global physical-car counts |
| `new_sample_status`, `rv_sample_status_age_N` | Sample labels in the updated macro; RV label considers both samples |
| `no_used_sample` | No qualifying used sample at that age |
| `no_new_baseline` | No qualifying new sample |
| `thin_new_baseline` / `thin_used_sample` | Corresponding sample below 11 in the updated macro |
| `review_ratio_above_100` | Used median exceeds the new median after the sample checks |
| `eligible` | Sample and ratio guard pass; not an independent comparability certification |
| `report_eligible_any_age` | At least one age has eligible RV; does not require a resolved class |
| `report_eligible_all_ages` | All four ages eligible and a resolved report class; deliberately much stricter |

The earlier threshold was **n ≥30**, not n >30. Earlier coverage counts such as 204 eligible groups or 11 complete rows belong to earlier windows/thresholds and must not be quoted as current after rebuilding. The “11 complete rows” referred to all-four-age groups, not 11 cars in the database.

Exactly **100% is allowed**. Above 100% remains review-flagged with displayed RV empty, while price medians remain available. Example: used median $22k / new median $20k =110%. That may reflect composition or market conditions and is not automatically invalid data or proven appreciation. The guard is a conservative report policy, not a mathematical upper bound. Do not change it without an explicit product decision.

The current baseline is `same_period_new_asking_price_v1`. A one-year-used fallback baseline discussed in the original brief is **not implemented in this macro**. Do not silently substitute it or label its ratio “relative to new.”

### Older historical prototype

It answers a different question from the same-period screenshot-style report. Keep it clearly separate; its historical status distribution is not the current calendar/rolling report coverage.

## 9. Segmentation and annual trends: safe use

### `marts.mart_market_segmentation`

Use `period_type = 'year'` or `'month'` deliberately. The main class table must filter **`location_scope = 'all'`**.

The mart includes own-class rows and a separately computed `vehicle_class = 'Всего'` total. It also contains `source_region` and `source_city` scopes. Do not add totals to class rows, or add different geographic scopes together. Overall medians are computed from observations; do not reconstruct them by averaging class medians.

Main fields include `vehicles_published`, `listing_publications`, `class_share_within_location_pct`, `price_sample_n`, `median_asking_price_usd`, `mean_vehicle_age_years`, `median_mileage_km`, electric/hybrid shares, and classification/exclusion counts. Age and mileage have separate sample sizes. Volume can include records without eligible prices; price exclusion does not mean the record was removed from every count.

Unresolved vehicle-level classes remain in their own bucket and in the share denominator. The RV consensus-label change does not make those source observations classified here. Classifying an RV group does not justify silently imputing that class back to all records.

Geography is based on raw source-qualified city/region labels and is not harmonized. Do not infer a verified national region comparison or combine ambiguous labels without a reviewed mapping.

### `marts.mart_market_trends`

Choose `summary_level` explicitly: `market`, `class`, or `model`. Do not add the three levels together. `listed_model_rank_in_class` ranks models by **vehicles with listing publications**, not confirmed sales.

The mart includes annual counts, prices, mean age, body shares, and powertrain shares. Body-based SUV share and electric share are overlapping dimensions and must not be summed as disjoint classes. A change in aggregate median price can reflect a change in model/age mix rather than a price movement for comparable cars.

2026 was partial in the supplied results. Compare complete years deliberately; do not compare a partial year with a full year as equivalent periods. Older complete calendar boundaries do not establish complete source capture.

Both marts leave active inventory, sold counts/shares, exposure days, and dealer share null with reason/status fields. Null does not mean zero. Sample layouts showing those columns do not establish that we can calculate them yet.
