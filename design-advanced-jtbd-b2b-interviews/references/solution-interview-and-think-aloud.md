# Solution interview and Think Aloud

Use this reference only to test a concrete offer: a landing page, sales deck, prototype, interface, PDF, pricing proposal, or comparable artifact. It does not replace a problem interview when the team still lacks evidence about real past behavior.

## Preconditions

- Define the segment, the need or job being tested, the concrete asset, and the business decision the session should inform.
- Choose a realistic exposure context: how the person would encounter the offer in actual work or a buying process.
- Define a next step the respondent can genuinely take. In B2B, a completed sale is uncommon and not the only useful signal.
- Do not test confidential data or ask the respondent to bypass company policy.

## Session flow

Use 45–60 minutes unless the user supplies a different constraint.

1. **Frame the relevant need (5–10 min).** Ground it in the respondent’s actual context. If past behavior is unknown, collect that before showing the concept.
2. **Set the exposure context (1 min).** For example: “Представьте, что вы увидели это как [роль] в момент [реальный контекст].” Do not over-explain value before the respondent encounters it.
3. **Start Think Aloud (first 5 min of interaction).** Ask the respondent to say everything they notice, understand, do not understand, expect, and consider doing. Demonstrate one short neutral example. Remind them gently and repeatedly during the first five minutes; silence is normal at first.
4. **Observe interaction (10–15 min).** Stay quiet except for neutral Think Aloud reminders. Record attention, confusion, interpretation, comparison, and action intent; do not coach through the asset.
5. **Debrief the offer (10–15 min).** Use the six questions below.
6. **Request a realistic costly action (5–10 min).** Make the request proportional to the person’s authority.
7. **Explore refusal or hesitation (5 min).** Treat it as data, not an objection to overcome.

## Debrief questions

Ask after the observed interaction, in this order when relevant:

1. “Решает ли это вашу потребность? Почему да или почему нет?”
2. “Какие сильные стороны вы увидели?”
3. “Какие слабые стороны, риски или непонятные места?”
4. “Если сравнить с тем, как вы решаете задачу сейчас или с альтернативами: в чём это выигрывает и проигрывает?”
5. “Соответствует ли предложенная цена ценности? Как вы бы это оценивали внутри компании?”
6. “Готовы ли вы перейти к следующему шагу сейчас? Какой шаг был бы реалистичен?”

Avoid the undiagnostic question “Вам нравится?”. It invites socially acceptable feedback rather than a decision-relevant trade-off.

## Costly actions in B2B

Select one action that matches the respondent’s authority. State that “not now” is an acceptable response.

| Respondent position | Appropriate action |
| --- | --- |
| Economic buyer or decision-maker | Schedule a commercial or technical evaluation, initiate the approved procurement path, or agree to a paid/piloted next step within their policy |
| Champion or influencer | Introduce the researcher to the decision-maker or next role in the buying chain; send a real internal message proposing an evaluation |
| User without buying authority | Identify the actual buying path and, if comfortable, make an introduction or share the asset with the responsible role |

After a declined or delayed action, ask: “Почему вы не готовы идти дальше сейчас? Чего не хватило или что должно измениться?” Capture the exact barrier: trust, job fit, authority, timing, price, implementation, policy, evidence, or competitor lock-in.

## Interpret the signal

Separate the following:

- **Observed behavior:** what the person clicked, read, skipped, said while using the asset, or actually did.
- **Declared evaluation:** stated strengths, concerns, and intent.
- **Commitment signal:** a completed introduction, scheduled evaluation, internal message, documented procurement step, or another costly action within the person’s control.

Praise, general interest, and a hypothetical promise are weak signals. Failure to take the action does not by itself disprove the underlying job; it may instead reveal a timing, authority, trust, or delivery constraint.

## Think Aloud moderator prompts

Use sparingly and neutrally:

- “Пожалуйста, продолжайте проговаривать, что сейчас замечаете и о чём думаете.”
- “Что в этом месте вам понятно или непонятно?”
- “Что вы сейчас ожидаете увидеть или сделать дальше?”

Do not ask leading prompts such as “Вам понятно, что мы экономим время?” Do not fill silences with explanations.
