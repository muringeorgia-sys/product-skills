# Rehearsal and feedback

Use this route when the user provides an answer, asks for an interviewer role-play, or asks to shorten an answer.

## Review an answer

Assess only material points:

1. Is the goal named and are assumptions explicit?
2. Is segmentation or priority meaningful?
3. Is research or data proposed before selecting a solution?
4. Is one solution selected by a criterion rather than listed?
5. Is the target behavior connected to a primary metric, rather than an interface action being treated as value?
6. Is there a way to measure effect, a proportionate limiting metric, and a segment that could change the conclusion?
7. Are there invented figures, uncertain terms, or unnecessary detail?

Name one or two highest-value improvements first, then offer a stronger formulation. Do not rewrite the full answer without a request.

## Mock interview

Ask one question at a time. After the answer, give short feedback: what worked, what is missing, and the likely next question. Increase difficulty only after the core logic is clear.

## 90-second answer

Use: frame and goal -> key reasoning step -> chosen action -> validation or metric -> limitation or next step. Remove recruiting, implementation, and statistics detail when it does not change the decision.

For more specific feedback, read `mentor-rules.md`; do not open full transcripts in a normal session.
