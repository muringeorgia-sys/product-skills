# Discovery and interviews

Use this route to plan a problem interview, solution interview, research study, recruiting, or synthesis.

## Select the route

- **Problem interview:** reconstruct real past behavior, problem, alternatives, and constraints.
- **Solution interview:** test response and next step for a concrete prototype, pitch, price, or offer.
- **Hybrid:** separate past-fact collection from response to a solution; do not show the concept before reconstructing past experience.

## Required frame

Define the decision the research must support; respondent segment and role; recent real episode; awareness of the job and solution; and needed result after research.

For B2B, distinguish user, initiator, influencer, chooser, budget approver, payer, implementer, and support when those roles materially differ.

## Output

Provide a research question, assumptions, inclusion/exclusion criteria, screener, duration, neutral-question sequence, observations to capture, synthesis coding, and decision rule.

In a problem interview, begin with the Russian equivalent of: "Recall the last time this happened. What happened first?" Ask about real actions and tradeoffs, not hypothetical desire.

In a solution interview, add Think Aloud, unprompted observation, comparison with the current way, and a realistic next step within the respondent's authority. Rejection is a useful signal, not an objection to overcome.
